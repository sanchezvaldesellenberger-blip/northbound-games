Help

Good questions

Do I need to download anything?

No. The arcade games run in your browser on the Games page and Deadzone runs in its own tab. There is nothing to install and no account to make.

Can I use a controller?

Yes. Connect a gamepad and press any button. The d-pad or left stick browses the games, A plays, and LB and RB switch tabs. In a game the d-pad moves, A is the main action and Start pauses.

Do the games work on my phone?

The six arcade games support touch. Turn your phone sideways for a bigger screen. Deadzone needs a desktop browser with a keyboard and mouse.

What is the Deadzone offline edition?

Deadzone is Northbound's 3D first-person shooter in development. The edition on this site runs entirely in your browser: bot matches on six maps, free roam and the training range. Online multiplayer is part of the full version. The first visit downloads about 100 MB.

Where are my scores saved?

Your personal bests and trophies stay in this browser, on this device. Online score checks use anonymous run records. If you choose “Post score” after a run, your nickname and score are sent to Northbound's online leaderboard and become public. No account or email is needed.

Reset progress only clears local trophies and scores. Clearing site data also creates a new leaderboard identity; it does not remove scores already posted. To request removal of a public entry, contact sanchez@sidequestdigital.net.

How is Northbound connected to SideQuestDigital?

Northbound Games is the game studio of SideQuestDigital. You can see SideQuestDigital's web design work at sidequestdigital.net.
