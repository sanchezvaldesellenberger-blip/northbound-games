Six maps

Where you'll fight

From a desert proving ground to a drowned village. Every map has windows to hold, flanks to loop and tight interiors to clear.

Four modes

How you'll play

Team deathmatch

You and four allied bots against five hostiles. First team to 100, or the lead when 8 minutes run out.

Search and destroy

Plant or defuse at bomb sites A and B. One life per round, and the first team to 4 rounds wins.

Domination

Capture and hold flags A, B and C. First to 200 wins, and the sides swap at halftime.

Free roam

Any map to yourself, to learn the routes, the sightlines and every ledge you can climb.

Features

Built like a console shooter

The parts that make it feel right, running in a browser tab.

  • Movement with momentumSprint, a short punchy slide and mantling: vault low cover and fences, or climb ledges up to about 2.1 m.
  • Sixteen gunsAssault rifles, SMGs, shotguns, LMGs, sniper rifles and pistols, from the MK9 Wasp to the LR-7 Sentinel, with optics, suppressors, foregrips and extended mags.
  • EquipmentFrag grenades and throwing knives, flashbangs and smoke.
  • Bots that play the mapThey path across every map, react with a human delay, strafe, reload and share sightings with their team.
  • Physics and handsOperators fall as jointed ragdolls, and fingers grip each weapon by its real surface through every reload.
  • Real renderingSun shadows, ambient occlusion, bloom and a film grade, with four quality presets from Performance to Ultra.

Training

The Yard

An indoor training facility with firing lanes and nine target operators. Try every weapon and attachment before you drop into a match.

Development

Update history

Deadzone is built in the open, one version at a time.

  1. 0.5Latest
    Home screen, movement, grips and animation

    A lobby with your operator on a lit stage. Faster, tighter movement with a landing slowdown that stops bunny-hopping. Mantling over cover and up ledges. Fingers that grip each weapon's real surface, and new crouch animation authored in Blender.

  2. 0.4
    Rendering, controls and HUD

    Bots that path, strafe and share sightings. Sky, sun shadows, ambient occlusion and bloom with four quality presets. Raw mouse input, an FOV slider and ADS sensitivity. A new HUD with a killfeed, minimap, hit markers and a killcam.

  3. 0.3
    Movement, weapon handling and death

    Momentum movement and the slide. Sleeves and gloved hands that move with the weapon and animate every reload. Operators that fall as physics ragdolls.

  4. 0.2
    Original characters and an expanded Yard

    Recon, Breacher and Sentry, original modular soldiers with their own equipment. A bigger Yard with the Foundry, a control room and a water tower.
